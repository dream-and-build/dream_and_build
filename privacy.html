Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

"Dream and Build publisher" is used only by the author of Dream & Build to upload the author's own videos to the author's own accounts.

What data is used: only the access tokens that the author grants to the tool for the author's own accounts, and basic account information (such as the account name) needed to confirm that videos go to the right account.

What is not collected: the tool does not collect, store or share data about any other users, viewers or followers.

Storage: tokens are stored only on the author's own computer and are never shared with third parties.

Deletion: access can be revoked at any time in the settings of each platform; the tool then stops working.
